# Bookshelf

Status: in progress
Started: 2027-01
Finished:

## Goal

A 3-shelf bookshelf for the office, 36" wide × 48" tall, from one sheet of 3/4" plywood.

Plans: [`plans.pdf`](plans.pdf). Shelf bracket model for the 3D printer: [`shelf-bracket.stl`](shelf-bracket.stl).

## Materials

| Item | Qty | Cost | Source |
|------|-----|------|--------|
| 3/4" plywood, 4×8 | 1 sheet | $62 | Home center |
| 1-1/4" pocket screws | 1 box | $8 | Home center |
| Iron-on edge banding | 1 roll | $12 | Home center |

## Tools used

[Acme TS10 table saw](../../tools/acme-ts10/) for breaking down the plywood, [Acme DD20 drill](../../tools/acme-dd20/) for pocket screws.

## Notes & lessons

Cut the sides first, then rip the shelves from what's left, so the grain runs the long way on every piece.
